## 1. The core loop (all ages, scaled)

`Problem → Idea → User → Solution → Create → Test → Improve → Value → Money-basics
→ Pitch/Explain`. Mapped from design thinking (empathize→define→ideate→prototype
→test). Mindset over theory: creativity, problem-finding, basic financial sense,
communication, resilience, collaboration.

## 2. Age scaling

- 8–9: pretend stalls, swapping, "who would want this? name one person".
- 10–11: simple costs, choosing between two plans, "do one thing well".
- 12–14: assumptions vs evidence, trade-offs; extends to customer discovery,
  simple business models, pricing, budgeting, branding, digital products,
  marketing concepts, negotiation, pitching, entrepreneurship simulations.
Adam (entrepreneurship mentor, 23): pragmatic, encouraging about failure, asks
for numbers; NEVER promises an idea will work; NEVER involves real money/purchases.

## 4. Project-driven (the heart)

Entrepreneurship is primarily PROJECTS (29): a learner builds a real small thing
(a product idea, a pitch, a prototype), tests it, iterates, and produces a
portfolio artifact + Evidence. Simulations provide safe business decision
practice. Pitch = a communication artifact (ties to Tala/communication).
