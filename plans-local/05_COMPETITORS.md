## 2. Product/pedagogy teardown (what each does; what it misses)

- **Duolingo** — strongest at: habit loop (streaks, bite-size), gamified
  retention, now AI roleplay (Max). Misses: real projects, portfolio/evidence a
  parent trusts, cross-domain, deep writing/creation. Lesson for USAM: borrow the
  habit cadence, but anchor it to **mastery + evidence**, not streak-for-streak's-sake.
- **Khanmigo** — strongest at: Socratic AI that withholds answers (safety-rated
  above raw ChatGPT), parent oversight (chat history, safety alerts, up to 10
  kids). Lesson: USAM's Azouz/mentors MUST be hints-before-answers and
  parent-inspectable — this is now a category expectation, not a differentiator.
- **Khan Academy (Kids)** — strongest at: free breadth, trusted brand. Misses:
  character-driven world, voice-first early-learner UX, entrepreneurship/AI
  creation. Lesson: USAM competes on **experience + identity (a world you return
  to)**, not on being another free content library.
- **Scratch / code.org / Tynker** — strongest at: block→text coding progression,
  creativity. Lesson: USAM coding should reuse this proven progression
  (blocks → script peek → real code) and real execution (Pyodide/Sandpack already
  in the stack), not reinvent it.

## 3. What USAM can do better (the differentiation thesis)

1. **One coherent world across 4 domains** (English + Coding + AI +
   Entrepreneurship) with ONE companion (Azouz) orchestrating — not four separate
   apps. No major competitor unifies these four for 8–14 with a character universe.
2. **Evidence a parent actually trusts** — mastery-by-competency + a portfolio of
   real created artifacts + verifiable credentials, not just "streak = 40 days".
3. **AI-native but safe-by-design** — characters that teach via Socratic hints,
   are parent-inspectable, never create dependency/secrecy (directive §31).
4. **Arabic-first + EG-Arabic voice** — a genuinely under-served market vs the
   English-centric incumbents.
5. **Entrepreneurship as a real pillar** — almost no 8–14 competitor treats
   build-a-thing / pitch / value as a first-class domain.

## 4. Category expectations USAM must MEET (table stakes, not differentiators)

Free tier to try · daily habit cadence · AI tutor that doesn't just hand answers
· parent oversight + safety alerts · family plan (multi-child) · offline-ish
reliability · measurable progress. These are the floor; §3 is the ceiling.
